Scored to convergence, not to taste

Every card in a replication is scored against the measured reference and driven to its own ceiling. The build stops when a card reaches 99% or when the numbers stop moving, never when the frame merely looks right.

Stop condition

A card at 99% of ceiling is done. Do not keep tuning it.

A card that survives three changes without moving more than 0.5 point is telling you the model is wrong, not the values. Go and measure which element owns the error before changing anything else.
